Emivita — six palettes, twelve themes

Six palettes, each a light and a dark theme, drawn for the Health / Consumer Wellbeing register: warm rather than clinical, and non-alarming by construction — red is quarantined to genuine failures, and every other state gets a tone that informs without startling.

What this is

  • Shipped, not proposed. These six replaced the previous eight outright and are on master (§7 R56). This page is the visual record of what the app renders, not a pitch for a change still to come.
  • Derived, not off-the-shelf. A ten-theme catalogue was available. A set of six that must share one register and stay mutually distinguishable isn’t something a catalogue answers, so these are derived from the Health / Consumer Wellbeing register instead.
  • Two roles are new. attention and neutralFill were split out of error, which had been painting a missed dose, a past-due day dot, and a note about an optional barcode scanner. They ride on LocalEmivitaColors, since all 22 of Material3’s slots were spoken for.
  • Chosen by search, not by eye. The token assignment and the two new colours are the winner of an optimization-engines run — fully computable spec, zero model judgements — scoring 0.9265, beating the runner-up assignment by 0.15.
  • Numbers are measured, and now enforced. Every ratio came from designkit — WCAG relative luminance and CIEDE2000, with colour-blindness simulated rather than assumed. All 132 hexes are asserted against the running app by PaletteSpecConsistencyTest, so this page, Color.kt and the spec cannot drift apart without a red build.

Tokens, measured

Text rows are measured against their own ground. Fill rows are measured against their derived label colour — what a button owes you is a readable label, not a fill that would also work as body text.

What keeps the register non-alarming

Three ordinary events are painted in the alarm red or in a colour that overstates them. Splitting one overloaded token fixes all three.
